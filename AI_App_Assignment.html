  • Highest-sales-value product categories;

6. Business Recommendations¶

Focus on categories that are doing well The company should continue to provide its products in categories that generate a good amount of sales value and run focused promotions.

Enhance delivery reliability

The analysis shows that lower review scores associated with late delivery, hence logistics performance should be prioritized. The company should monitor late delivery rates by seller and location.

Look out for low-rated categories In categories where review scores remain low, product quality, seller performance, descriptions and delivery issues should all be investigated.

Give rewards to successful sellers High sales - value sellers with high customer ratings could be benchmarked against marketplace best practices.

Check out regional opportunities Potential expansion opportunities would be areas with lower sales value but marketing investment should be supported by further analysis of the customer, logistics and purchasing behavior.

7. Limitations¶

This analysis has several limitations.

First, the dataset covers a historical period from 2016 to 2018, so the findings should not automatically be generalised to the current e-commerce market.

Second, the analysis is explanatory rather than causal. Relationships between delivery, freight, reviews and sales do not prove that one variable directly causes another.

Third, some orders do not have complete delivery information. Therefore, delivery-time analysis focuses on delivered orders.

Finally, customer review scores are subjective and may not capture every aspect of customer experience.
